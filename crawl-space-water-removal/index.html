Crawl Space Water Removal Help in Lehigh Acres

Water under the house is easy to miss. The usual clues are chilly, clammy floors, an earthy odor rising from the registers, or a pool of water waiting behind the access door. At that point the batts are typically saturated and the framing overhead has sat damp for several days. Crews from our company extract the water, haul away anything that cannot be saved and dry the structure ahead of rot.

Getting the water out is only the first half. Because lumber under a floor is slow to soak and slow to dry, our crew tracks its moisture content with a meter and runs dehumidification for the 3 to 5 days it normally takes to bring the readings down. Our team traces the source too, whether that is a supply line leak, a drain, or rain driven groundwater, so the space stays dry afterward.

What It Looks Like

  • An increase in insects and pests that like damp, dark spaces
  • Hardwood boards upstairs starting to cup, with edges higher than the centers
  • A musty, earthy smell that is strongest near the floor vents
  • Flooring overhead that seems chilly, clammy, spongy or springy underfoot
  • A look through the hatch shows pooled water or soupy soil
  • Sweating pipes and ductwork that drip onto the ground beneath the home
How We Fix It

How We Handle Crawl Space Water Removal

1

Inspect and Make Safe

Before a technician goes under the house, we look for live electrical dangers, fallen ductwork and dirty water. Then we map how far the water reached and take moisture readings on the joists and subfloor.

2

Remove Standing Water

Standing water comes out with submersible pumps and hoses, down to the footing trenches and dips where it gathers. Any silt or mud sitting on the liner gets removed during this step.

3

Clear Out Ruined Material

Soaked insulation, ruined vapor barrier and debris are bagged and carried out. Where mold has begun or is likely, the bare joists and subfloor get cleaned and an antimicrobial applied.

4

Dry and Verify

A dehumidifier and low profile air movers stay running beneath the floor until the lumber meters at a normal level. We recheck with a meter and give you the readings and a prevention plan.

What Happens if You Wait

First day

Puddles Form Underneath

The dirt floor starts absorbing moisture as puddles form in every dip. Insulation starts wicking it up and humidity under the floor climbs fast.

24 to 48 hours

Mold Gets Started

Paper faced insulation and moist joists are where mold is able to appear first. The weight of the water drags the batts loose from the underside of the subfloor.

Days 3 through 7

Moisture Moves Upstairs

Damp air rises into the living space. You might see floorboards cupping and notice a musty odor while the framing continues to soak.

After a week

Rot Sets In

Lumber left damp this long starts breaking down. Expect softer framing, rusted fasteners, and a wet environment that draws in termites and similar pests.

Behind the Surface

The Damage You Do Not See

Wet Wiring and Ducts

Most crawl spaces carry wiring, junction boxes and HVAC ducts. When duct insulation is saturated it sends stale air around the home, while flooded wiring creates a real risk of shock.

What You Breathe Upstairs

Much of what you breathe on the main level drifts up from beneath the house, carrying any mold spores and musty smells along with it.

Pests Drawn to Moisture

Damp wood and wet soil are exactly what termites, carpenter ants and rodents look for. Once the area is dry it becomes far less inviting.

Shifting Piers and Supports

When the ground stays soaked, footings may lose support and piers can tilt or sink. Doors that stick and floors that slope upstairs are possible signs.
