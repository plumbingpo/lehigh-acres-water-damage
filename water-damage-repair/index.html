A dry house is not yet a finished house. Once the moisture readings are back to normal, there are still cut walls, missing flooring and bare subfloor to deal with. Rebuilding all of that is what water damage repair means. No two repair jobs are the same size. A minor loss can need just a couple of feet of wallboard plus baseboard and a coat of paint. How to Prevent Water Damage Repair Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Give fresh paint, caulk and flooring adhesive time to cure before wet mopping or heavy use.
  • Take pictures of damaged materials prior to removal, since the insurer may want them later.
  • Ask for final moisture readings before any contractor closes up a wall or floor.
  • Laundry rooms, bathrooms and kitchens should be rebuilt with moisture resistant wallboard and proper backer board.
  • Save spare tile and flooring, plus paint color details, for matching later repairs.
  • Run the bath fan during showers and for a while afterward so rebuilt walls stay dry.

Signs You Need Water Damage Repair in Lehigh Acres

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.
  • Following water from above, part of the ceiling droops, has cracks or crumbles when touched.
  • You see brown stains, peeling or bubbles in paint along the path the water took.
  • Baseboards and door casings are warped or no longer sit flat against the wall.
  • Since the water damage, certain doors drag on the jamb or miss the latch.
  • Cabinet bases or side panels are swollen, soft or peeling at the bottom.
